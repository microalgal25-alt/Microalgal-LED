The Global Challenge

1.81 billion people live in high-risk flood areas. Rural communities face repeated power outages with no viable solutions.

🌊 Devastating Floods

Monsoon seasons cause recurring water logging across rural regions. Families lose homes and livelihoods without mitigation infrastructure.

⚡ Energy Poverty

Millions lack electricity access. In villages like Pandanwatand, residents walk 6 km just to charge phones off truck batteries.

🔋 Grid Instability

Weak rural grids cannot support variable renewable inputs. No storage, no smart infrastructure—only rolling blackouts.

Our Elegant Solution

A decentralized, nature-inspired system that transforms water into reliable clean energy.

How MicroAlgal-LED Works

🚰 Harvest: Collect floodwater and wastewater into cultivator tanks—no waste, full potential.

🌿 Cultivate: Grow microalgae (Chlorella, Scenedesmus) in controlled conditions while absorbing CO₂ and purifying water.

💨 Convert: Feed harvested algae into anaerobic digesters to produce biogas (0.43–0.47 m³ CH₄/kg).

⚡ Generate: Power compact gensets supplying 24/7 electricity to homes, schools, and local industries.

90-Day Path to Launch

Our rapid prototyping timeline from site selection to full operational deployment.

Site & Permits

Secure 5×5 m sunlit location. Get school principal approval. Consult local environmental boards for compliance.

Procurement

Order bioreactor liners, barrels, and sensors. Collect starter algae cultures from local fisheries labs.

Build & Setup

Install pond liner, fill with wastewater, adjust pH to 7.5, begin automated monitoring.

First Harvest

Execute chitosan flocculation tests. Load digester with prepared slurry. Begin biogas production.

Gas Quality Test

Install iron-sponge scrubber. Run generator on 30% biogas blend. Monitor methane quality & pressure.

Data & Scale

Log all metrics (CH₄ %, H₂S, kWh output). Publish results. Prepare for site replication.
